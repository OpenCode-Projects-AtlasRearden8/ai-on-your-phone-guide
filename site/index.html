The AI-on-Your-Phone Guide

A friendly manual for using AI on the phone already in your pocket. No accounts, no API keys, no jargon walls.

Companion to the book The Massive AI Manual by Dylan Asdale, published by Asdale Dynamics, LLC.

Why this manual exists

Most AI guides are written for people sitting at a desk with a laptop. But most of us actually live on our phones. This manual is for the moments when you're in a waiting room, on lunch break at the nursing station, or lying in bed wondering how to phrase a tricky email.

Everything here is written like a friend would explain it: plain English, step by step, never condescending. If something can be done for free without creating an account, we say so. If it costs money or needs a login, we say that too.

How to use it

  • Read chapters in order the first time. After that, jump to whatever you need.
  • There's no quiz and no accounts required — the site runs entirely offline, from static files.
  • Print-friendly: every page has a print stylesheet, so Print → Save as PDF gives you a clean paper copy.

Who this is for

Anyone curious about AI, especially:

  • beginners who feel behind and don't want to be talked down to,
  • nurses, teachers, and shift workers who use phones more than laptops,
  • folks on limited data or older phones,
  • anyone who cares where their private stuff actually goes.

Education, not advice. This guide is educational. Nothing here is medical, legal, or professional advice — and check your employer's policy before using AI with anything work-related.
