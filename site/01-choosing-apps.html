Chapter 1. Choosing AI Apps

How to pick the right AI chat app, sidestep the copycats, and start simple.

The short version

If you want one answer before reading further: pick one well-known app from an official app store, use the free tier, and skip anything that promises you "unlimited GPT" from a name you've never heard of. Now, here's why.

Start from the official store

Install AI chat apps only from:

  • iPhone — the App Store
  • Android — Google Play (or the phone maker's own store)

The big names you'll see straight away: the apps made by the well-known AI labs and phone makers themselves. You don't need this book to name them for you — the point is the pattern:

  1. The developer name matches the AI company (or the phone company).
  2. The app has a long history of reviews, not 5,000 five-star reviews posted in the last two weeks.
  3. It doesn't demand access to your contacts, photos, and microphone the moment you open it — a chat app mainly needs a network connection and, if you want voice mode, the mic.

The copycat problem

Search any app store for "AI chat" and you'll get dozens of lookalike apps: unfamiliar logos, stock-photo icons, and screens pleading for a subscription. Some are worse than useless — they resell someone else's model at a markup, or quietly feed your conversations into ad networks.

Red flags worth tapping "Back" for:

  • "GPT-5 free forever" style promises from unknown developers — suspicious from both a legal and a technical angle.
  • Checkbox walls during setup asking for contacts, files, precise location. A chat app doesn't need these to work.
  • Reviews that all sound like the same person wrote them (uncanny, over-long, suspiciously good grammar).
  • No privacy policy, or one that's just a wall of generic text. Legit apps can describe, in one readable page, what gets stored and why.

Free vs paid

Almost every mainstream AI app has a free tier, and for phone-sized questions (emails, recipes, explanations, "help me phrase this"), free is enough most days. Paid tiers mainly buy:

  • bigger usage limits (good for heavy daily use),
  • access to the newest, smartest models,
  • extras like image generation or file uploads.

Advice: live on free for a month. If you hit the usage ceiling repeatedly, then consider paying — one month at a time, cancel-friendly, never a "yearly deal" from an app you met last week.

Naming confusion (it's not just you)

App stores are blurry. Snap-looking icons for "AI Chatbot", fake "official" badges, apps called things like "AI Assistant Pro" by developers named nothing you'd recognise. A quick sanity check: search for the real company's name on the web first. Does that company list its own app on its own website? If the maker's official site never mentions an app with that exact name, assume it's a mimic.

A few apps worth knowing by category

You don't need all of these. Start with one assistant, add tools as you need them.

Category What it does Worth trying because
General assistant Chat, questions, writing help The default place to start
Local (offline) assistant Runs on your phone, no internet needed Privacy plus no data usage
Voice-first assistant You talk, it talks back Good for driving, cooking, hands-busy
Phone-maker assistant Built into your phone's OS Already installed, no extra accounts

Chapter 2 explains what "local" really means and why it matters.

Uninstalling cleanly

Trying out an app and not liking it is normal. To leave cleanly, uninstall it the way you'd remove any app. Android: long-press the icon → Uninstall. iPhone: long-press the icon → Remove app → Delete app. Uninstalling also deletes the app's stored data on the phone.

And after you uninstall: if you gave an app an account or email, check that app's website for a "delete my account" option — deleting the app usually does not delete the account behind it.

Checkpoint

You should now be able to:

  • Explain why "official app store developer name matches" matters.
  • List three copycat red flags.
  • Say where the app's own uninstall instructions are, without opening the manual.
